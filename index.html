Our lab

Where cars get their personality.

iilumolab is a Keysborough workshop built by people who'd rather be in the garage. We started with lighting, the iilumo range of LED and ambient kits, and grew into full builds: carbon aero, exhausts, coilovers, brakes and the small details that make a car feel like yours.

Every install is done in house, by the team that sells you the part. No outsourcing, no guesswork.
